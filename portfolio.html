About

Two jobs, one habit

I'm a university student in Nigeria, studying social sciences by day and running a digital marketing and publishing practice on the side — not as a side hustle I'll drop after graduation, but as the actual plan.

On one track, I'm a certified Google Digital Marketer and Social Media Manager, doing freelance content strategy and social management for small and mid-sized businesses. On the other, I founded Unbreakable Mind, a self-improvement and money-mindset brand — eBooks, long-form content, and a YouTube channel — built entirely on free tools and consistency.

The thread between both: showing up on a schedule, writing in a voice that sounds like a person and not a template, and treating audiences as specific people rather than one generic feed.
